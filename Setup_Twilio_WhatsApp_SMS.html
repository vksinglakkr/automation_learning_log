📱 Twilio Se WhatsApp/SMS Bhejna Kaise Shuru KareinAccount SID, Auth Token aur Sandbox Activation

n8n WhatsApp ya SMS bhejne ke liye Twilio ka istemaal karta hai — Twilio ka apna Account SID aur Auth Token chahiye hota hai, jo free account banate hi mil jaate hain।

Steps

  1. twilio.com par ek free account banao
  2. Console Dashboard par seedhe "Account SID" aur "Auth Token" dikhega — Auth Token "hidden" hota hai, "click to reveal" karna padega
  3. Dono copy karke n8n ke Twilio credential me paste kar do

WhatsApp Sandbox Activate Karna (Zaroori Step)

  1. Twilio Console me "Messaging" → "Try it out" → "Send a WhatsApp message" me jao
  2. Ek sandbox number aur ek join-code milega, jaise:
join happy-tiger
  1. Apne khud ke WhatsApp se us sandbox number par yehi join-code bhejo
  2. Tabhi Twilio tumhare number par WhatsApp message bhej sakta hai — ye step bhoolne se message chup-chap fail ho jaata hai, koi error bhi nahi dikhta
n8n ke Twilio node me "From" field me sandbox number is format me daalna hota hai: whatsapp:+14155238886 — aur "To" me apna verified number, waise hi whatsapp: prefix ke saath।
